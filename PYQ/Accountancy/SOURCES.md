## Pattern changes to remember during the audit

- **2022–2024:** 50 questions, attempt any 40, 200 marks (2024 booklet: 60 min).
- **2025 onward:** 50 questions, all compulsory, 60 min, +5 / −1 (matches our papers). In 2025 NTA also added a choice between Unit 5 and an optional unit in Accountancy.
- For difficulty and pattern, weight the 2025 NTA paper highest. It is the only complete, official paper in the current format.
