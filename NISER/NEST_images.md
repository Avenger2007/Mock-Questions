# NEST — image prompts

One entry per question that needs a figure, in the format given in `NISER/NISER_Test_Instructions.md` (section 9, "Figures"). Keep entries in test and question order. Every entry must match a question with the same `image_reference`.


## NEST Test 2

### NEST_T02_Q03.png — Test 2, Q03 (Biology, logistic growth of yeast)
- **Purpose:** the student reads the carrying capacity and the point of fastest growth from a growth curve.
- **Size and style:** 1200 × 800 px, white background, black axes, one smooth dark-blue curve (3 px), light grey grid, sans-serif labels 24 px or larger; looks like a textbook figure.
- **Must show:** x-axis "Time (h)" from 0 to 20 with ticks every 2 h; y-axis "Number of yeast cells per microlitre" from 0 to 700 with ticks every 100. A smooth S-shaped curve through these points (time, cells): (0, 20), (2, 40), (4, 85), (6, 170), (8, 300), (10, 430), (12, 520), (14, 570), (16, 592), (18, 598), (20, 600). Small filled dots on the curve at each of these 11 points. The curve must be visibly flat from 16 h to 20 h and steepest around 8 h.
- **Labels:** axis titles only; caption under the graph: "Growth of yeast in a closed flask".
- **Must not show:** any horizontal line or label marking K, "carrying capacity", "K/2", "inflection", "lag/log/stationary phase", any tangent line, or any arrow pointing at 8 h or 300.
- **Alt text:** Graph of yeast cell density against time from 0 to 20 hours; the curve starts near 20, rises in an S shape and levels off at 600 cells per microlitre.

### NEST_T02_Q27.png — Test 2, Q27 (Chemistry, P–V cycle of an ideal gas)
- **Purpose:** the student reads the three corner points of a cycle and finds work in one step and heat for the whole cycle.
- **Size and style:** 1200 × 900 px, white background, black axes, cycle drawn in dark red (3 px) with arrowheads in the middle of each side, light grey grid, sans-serif labels 24 px or larger.
- **Must show:** x-axis "V / L" from 0 to 35 with ticks every 5; y-axis "p / bar" from 0 to 3.5 with ticks every 0.5. Three points marked with filled dots and labelled A at (10, 1), B at (10, 3), C at (30, 1). Straight segments A→B (vertical), B→C (straight slanted line), C→A (horizontal). Arrowheads show the direction A → B → C → A (up the left side, down along the slanted side, leftwards along the bottom). Thin dashed guide lines from each corner to both axes so that 10, 30, 1 and 3 can be read exactly.
- **Labels:** "A", "B", "C" next to the corners; axis titles only.
- **Must not show:** any shading of the enclosed area, any numbers for work or heat, the words "clockwise", "isochoric", "isobaric", or any value of the area.
- **Alt text:** Pressure–volume diagram of a triangular cycle with corners A at 10 L and 1 bar, B at 10 L and 3 bar, and C at 30 L and 1 bar, traversed A to B to C to A.

### NEST_T02_Q76.png — Test 2, Q76 (Physics, unequally spaced equipotential lines)
- **Purpose:** the student finds where the field is strongest from the spacing of equipotentials and finds the work done along a curved path.
- **Size and style:** 1400 × 800 px, white background, four solid black vertical lines (2 px), a horizontal x-axis along the bottom, sans-serif labels 24 px or larger; schematic, no shading.
- **Must show:** x-axis "x (cm)" from −1 to 7 with ticks every 1 cm. Four vertical lines, all of the same height, at x = 0, 1, 3 and 6 cm, labelled at the top "40 V", "30 V", "20 V" and "10 V" respectively. Point P (filled dot, labelled "P") on the 40 V line about one-third of the way up; point Q (filled dot, labelled "Q") on the 10 V line about two-thirds of the way up. A smooth dashed curve from P to Q that first bulges upward and then comes down (clearly not a straight line), with a small arrowhead pointing towards Q.
- **Labels:** the four potentials, "P", "Q", axis title.
- **Must not show:** any electric-field arrows or field lines, any value of E, any "+" or "−" charges, the word "work", or any distance arrows between the lines.
- **Alt text:** Four vertical equipotential lines labelled 40 V at x = 0 cm, 30 V at x = 1 cm, 20 V at x = 3 cm and 10 V at x = 6 cm, with point P on the 40 V line, point Q on the 10 V line and a curved dashed path from P to Q.

### NEST_T02_Q80.png — Test 2, Q80 (Physics, refraction angle against incidence angle)
- **Purpose:** the student reads the critical angle from where the curve ends at r = 90°.
- **Size and style:** 1200 × 900 px, white background, black axes, one smooth dark-blue curve (3 px), light grey grid, sans-serif labels 24 px or larger.
- **Must show:** x-axis "Angle of incidence i in medium A (degrees)" from 0 to 90 with ticks every 10 and an extra labelled tick at 42; y-axis "Angle of refraction r in medium B (degrees)" from 0 to 90 with ticks every 10. A smooth curve through (i, r): (0, 0), (10, 15.0), (20, 30.7), (30, 48.3), (35, 59.0), (40, 73.8), (41, 78.6), (42, 90). The curve ends at (42, 90) with a small open circle; nothing is drawn for i > 42. A thin dashed vertical guide line from (42, 0) to (42, 90). A thin dotted grey line r = i from (0, 0) to (90, 90) for comparison, labelled "r = i".
- **Labels:** axis titles, the "42" tick, "r = i" on the dotted line.
- **Must not show:** the words "critical angle", "total internal reflection", any refractive index value, or any ray diagram.
- **Alt text:** Graph of refraction angle r against incidence angle i for light passing from medium A to medium B; the curve rises from the origin, steepens, and ends at r = 90 degrees when i = 42 degrees, with no curve beyond i = 42 degrees.
