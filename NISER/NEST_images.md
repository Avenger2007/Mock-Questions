# NEST — image prompts

One entry per question that needs a figure, in the format given in `NISER/NISER_Test_Instructions.md` (section 9, "Figures"). Keep entries in test and question order. Every entry must match a question with the same `image_reference`.


## NEST Test 2

### NEST_T02_Q03.png — Test 2, Q03 (Biology, logistic growth of yeast)
- **Purpose:** the student reads the carrying capacity and the point of fastest growth from a growth curve.
- **Size and style:** 1200 × 800 px, white background, black axes, one smooth dark-blue curve (3 px), light grey grid, sans-serif labels 24 px or larger; looks like a textbook figure.
- **Must show:** x-axis "Time (h)" from 0 to 20 with ticks every 2 h; y-axis "Number of yeast cells per microlitre" from 0 to 700 with ticks every 100. A smooth S-shaped curve through these points (time, cells): (0, 20), (2, 40), (4, 85), (6, 170), (8, 300), (10, 430), (12, 520), (14, 570), (16, 592), (18, 598), (20, 600). Small filled dots on the curve at each of these 11 points. The curve must be visibly flat from 16 h to 20 h and steepest around 8 h.
- **Labels:** axis titles only; caption under the graph: "Growth of yeast in a closed flask".
- **Must not show:** any horizontal line or label marking K, "carrying capacity", "K/2", "inflection", "lag/log/stationary phase", any tangent line, or any arrow pointing at 8 h or 300.
- **Alt text:** Graph of yeast cell density against time from 0 to 20 hours; the curve starts near 20, rises in an S shape and levels off at 600 cells per microlitre.

### NEST_T02_Q27.png — Test 2, Q27 (Chemistry, P–V cycle of an ideal gas)
- **Purpose:** the student reads the three corner points of a cycle and finds work in one step and heat for the whole cycle.

- **Must show:** x-axis "V / L" from 0 to 35 with ticks every 5; y-axis "p / bar" from 0 to 3.5 with ticks every 0.5. Thin dashed guide lines from each corner to both axes so that 10, 30, 1 and 3 can be read exactly.

- **Must not show:** any shading of the enclosed area, any numbers for work or heat, the words "clockwise", "isochoric", "isobaric", or any value of the area.

### NEST_T02_Q76.png — Test 2, Q76 (Physics, unequally spaced equipotential lines)

- **Must not show:** any electric-field arrows or field lines, any value of E, any "+" or "−" charges, the word "work", or any distance arrows between the lines.

## NEST Test 3

### NEST_T03_Q11.png — Test 3, Q11 (Biology, transverse sections of a dicot and a monocot root)
- **Purpose:** the student counts xylem and phloem patches and notes the pith to tell a dicot root from a monocot root.
- **Size and style:** 1600 × 800 px, white background, two circular sections side by side, clean line-art in the style of a textbook diagram (black outlines, xylem patches filled dark red, phloem patches filled green, pith pale yellow, cortex unshaded with faint cell outlines), sans-serif labels 24 px or larger.
- **Must show:** Left section labelled "X" underneath: outer epidermis, wide cortex, a thin endodermis ring and pericycle; inside it exactly 4 xylem patches (wedge-shaped, pointing outwards) alternating with exactly 4 phloem patches around the centre; the xylem wedges meet near the centre so that there is no pith. Right section labelled "Y" underneath: same outer layers; inside the endodermis exactly 14 small xylem patches alternating with exactly 14 phloem patches arranged in a ring; a large central pith filling the middle. One leader line on each section labelled "xylem", one labelled "phloem", and on Y one labelled "pith".
- **Labels:** "X", "Y", "xylem", "phloem", "pith", "endodermis".
- **Must not show:** the words "dicot", "monocot", "diarch/tetrarch/polyarch", any count written on the figure, or any secondary growth (no cambium ring).
- **Alt text:** Two transverse sections of young roots side by side; section X has four xylem patches alternating with four phloem patches and no pith, section Y has many xylem patches alternating with phloem patches around a large central pith.

## NEST Test 5

### NEST_T05_Q03.png — Test 5, Q03 (Biology, three-generation pedigree)
- **Purpose:** the student deduces the mode of inheritance and the genotypes from the pattern of affected and unaffected individuals.
- **Size and style:** 1400 × 900 px, white background, standard pedigree symbols drawn in black (2 px lines): squares for males, circles for females, filled solid black for affected and white for unaffected; horizontal marriage lines, vertical descent lines and a sibship line; sans-serif labels 24 px or larger.
- **Must show:** Generation labels "I", "II", "III" at the left. Generation I: an unfilled square labelled "1" joined by a marriage line to an unfilled circle labelled "2". Generation II (children of I-1 × I-2, left to right): a filled circle "1", an unfilled square "2", a filled square "3". To the left of II-1, an unfilled square labelled "4", joined to II-1 by a marriage line (II-4 has no parents drawn). Generation III (children of II-1 × II-4): an unfilled square "1" and an unfilled circle "2".
- **Labels:** "I", "II", "III" and the individual numbers under each symbol.
- **Must not show:** genotypes, half-filled (carrier) symbols, dots inside symbols, the words "recessive", "dominant", "autosomal", "X-linked" or "carrier", or any key other than "filled = affected".
- **Alt text:** Three-generation pedigree. Generation I: an unaffected man (I-1) and an unaffected woman (I-2). Generation II: their children are an affected daughter (II-1), an unaffected son (II-2) and an affected son (II-3); II-1 is married to an unaffected man (II-4). Generation III: II-1 and II-4 have an unaffected son (III-1) and an unaffected daughter (III-2).
