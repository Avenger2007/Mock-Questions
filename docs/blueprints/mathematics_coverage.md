## B. Depth focus

| Tests | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | NCERT exercise level; standard functions; small integers; one trap per item |
| 6–10 | two-step items, mixed units within one item (e.g. matrix inverse inside a system, monotonicity of an integral function), less familiar numbers |
| 11–15 | hardest NCERT-level reasoning: Bayes with three causes, shortest distance with parameters, linear DEs needing substitution, EMI/bond/sinking fund chains, t-test with computed mean and SD |
