# Blueprint — CUET (UG) 2027 Hindi (102)

**Syllabus:** `CUET SYLLABUS/md/Hindi - 102.md` (NTA, "for the year 2026", the latest published). It has two areas:
- **Reading comprehension:** three types of passage of at most 300 words: factual, narrative and literary.
- **Verbal ability:** rearranging the parts, match the following, choosing the correct word, and synonyms and antonyms.

The syllabus says questions are "not limited to" these topics. NTA's actual Hindi papers are mostly व्याकरण and शब्द-ज्ञान (see §2), so those topics are in scope.

**Series:** 15 tests, `CUET/Hindi/CUET HINDI TEST 1–15.json`.

**What this means for OurMocks:**
- About 80% of the paper tests grammar and word knowledge, so the verbal-ability share here is larger than in English.
- Match lists, sequences and multi-selects ("केवल (A), (B) और (D)") come up 12–15 times per paper. Every part of them must be right.
- Passage sets mix comprehension with language questions on words taken from the passage.
- A little Hindi literary history (writer ↔ work, period) is asked every year.

## 4. Difficulty (every test)

**8 easy / 32 medium / 10 hard.** The rough split is RC 3 / 9 / 3 and VA 5 / 23 / 7 (±1).

- **Easy:** a stated detail; a common synonym, antonym or idiom; a basic प्रत्यय; a plain dictionary order.
- **Medium:** an inference from one or two sentences; a less common synonym; a संधि or समास of a standard word; a spelling trap (ज्ज्व, ई / इ); a match list.
- **Hard:** tone, figure of speech or the central idea in a literary passage; कारक or वाच्य where two readings look possible; an advanced antonym; a multi-select or statements item where every part must be checked; अलंकार in a classical line.

## 6. Passages

- **All passages are original**, written for OurMocks, and at most 300 words.
  - **Factual passages** give accurate, checkable information on science, environment, society or Indian heritage.
  - **Narrative passages** tell a story or a memoir.
  - **Literary passages** alternate: verse (पद्यांश) in odd tests, poetic prose in even tests. They are rich in imagery and अलंकार.
- **Question mix in each set:**
  - one detail
  - one inference or the writer's view
  - one word, phrase or मुहावरा in context
  - one central idea or title
  - one language question on a word or line of the passage (संधि, समास, अलंकार, वर्ण-क्रम, पर्यायवाची), as NTA sets them
- No two tests share a passage theme (see the coverage map).

## 7. Key and order

- **Answer key:**
  - 13 / 12 / 13 / 12 per test, rotated.
  - No letter more than 3 times in a row.
  - The correct option is never clearly the longest.
- **Sequence items:** the correct order is never (A), (B), (C), (D).
- **Multi-selects:** each distractor fails on exactly one statement.
- **Question order:** `shuffle_test.py` puts questions in random exam order and keeps each passage set together.

## 8. Language standard

- **Standard Hindi:** the spelling of the केंद्रीय हिंदी निदेशालय (मानक वर्तनी), with अनुस्वार for nasal consonants, as in NCERT books (हिंदी, संस्कृति).
- **Grammar** follows NCERT's व्याकरण and the standard reference grammars (कामताप्रसाद गुरु, *हिंदी व्याकरण*).
- **Literary history** follows आचार्य रामचंद्र शुक्ल, *हिंदी साहित्य का इतिहास*, and NCERT.
- **Unambiguous answers:** every item has exactly one defensible answer.
  - Words whose समास is disputed (नीलकंठ, पीतांबर, दशानन, which can be कर्मधारय, द्विगु or बहुव्रीहि) are not used as single-answer items.
  - Antonyms with more than one acceptable answer are not used. This was the audit's finding on the old सत्कार item.
- **No repeats:** no target word, मुहावरा, संधि / समास word or literary example is tested twice in the series. The coverage map keeps a ledger.

## 9. Explanations

- **Structure:** `उत्तर:` → `क्यों:` → `अन्य विकल्प क्यों नहीं:` → `मुख्य बिंदु:`.
- **Passage questions** quote or point to the line that decides the answer.
- **Grammar items** give the rule: the संधि rule with its vowel change, the समास-विग्रह, the कारक sign and its role.
- **Vocabulary items** give the meaning of the answer and of each distractor.

## 10. Out of scope (never tested)

- Questions on specific NCERT chapters (आरोह, वितान): CUET Hindi 102 has no prescribed texts.
- Grammar terms for their own sake, beyond what NTA asks.
- Disputed literary dates.
- Very rare Sanskrit words that no general reader would meet.
