# COMEDK UGET past-paper analysis

## What was examined

The [source register](../../PYQ/COMEDK/SOURCES.md) covers downloads from three years. The 2026 excerpts were read in full. The 2025 and 2024 documents were sampled, with image-dependent material separated from plain-text extraction. Treat this as qualitative calibration. There is no reliable chapter-frequency estimate, difficulty distribution, figure frequency, exact subject sequence, or shift weighting from this corpus. The 2026 excerpts print full-exam boilerplate but contain 25 items combined.

The two recall excerpts contain 11 Mathematics, 9 Physics and 5 Chemistry items by subject matter. This includes flawed, ambiguous or possibly out-of-scope reconstructions. These counts describe only the two PDFs. At least the following flaws explain why presence in a recall document cannot establish scope or a valid answer:

- R26-AM Q8 gives a speed and stopping time without enough information about the intervening motion. Do not assume constant speed or uniform deceleration without a condition.
- R26-AM Q10 uses overlapping magnet descriptions. Build mutually exclusive options.
- R26-AM Q13 calls for a phase-rule result outside the printed Chemistry scope used here.
- R26-PM Q5 needs a stated sampling convention. Its printed fractions also warrant source checking.
- R25-A puts Mathematics under a Physics heading and loses some choices and diagrams in the text layer. Use content-based tagging and the PDF page, never the section heading alone.
- Recall items on AP sums, affine means, or extended parametric areas can suggest pacing without authorising those exact demands under the printed 2026 scope.

The recurring design signal is breadth under time pressure, with direct conceptual recognition, short calculations, and a smaller set of tightly scoped multi-step tasks. Comparative choices, interpreted diagrams and structure-based chemistry deserve representation. The available evidence does not support official quotas for these styles. Start with house budgets in the plan and calibrate with timed independent attempts.

## Expansion protocol

Before representing any chapter weighting as empirical, tag a defined number of complete read-throughs across multiple recent shifts. Track original pages and extraction problems, scope and excluded counts, dominant task and secondary skills, image necessity, independent solve result, and measured timing if available. Report the denominator by subject and by session. Compare source overlap and conflicting keys. Retain separate metrics for authentically sourced papers, reproductions, and recall samples. A third-party paper or key never overrides the official syllabus.
