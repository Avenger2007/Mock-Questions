# Coverage map — CUET Geography, Tests 1–15

Written **before** any test, so that repeats are prevented rather than fixed. Rules:
- Every case theme is used **once** in the series (§A).
- Each **answer fact** (the fact a question turns on, and each correct pair of a match item) is a short key such as `jhuming`, `kudremukh iron ore`, `trans-siberian terminals`. A key may be used **at most twice in the series**, in different tests and different formats. Before a test is shipped its keys are checked against the ledgers in §C (`geo_check.py`, flag `REPEAT-TARGET`) and then added to them (`mark_geo.py`). Distractors may repeat.
- Every test covers every unit in the quota of the blueprint (§3).

## B. Slot rotation

- **A1** alternates definitions and approaches (environmental determinism, possibilism, neo-determinism; welfare, radical, behavioural schools; branches and sister disciplines).
- **A4** rotates gathering, pastoral nomadism and ranching, agriculture types (at least two each test), and mining.
- **A7** rotates roads, railways, waterways (canals and inland routes), ocean routes, air transport, pipelines and communication.
- **B5** rotates metallic minerals, non-metallic minerals, coal and petroleum, nuclear, and non-conventional energy.
- **B7 / B8** rotate roads, railways, waterways, airways, pipelines, communication, foreign trade, seaports and airports.
