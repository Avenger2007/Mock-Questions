# COMEDK Tests 10 and 11 review

Each paper contains 180 single-answer questions, with 60 each in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics. Each subject uses 20 Class XI and 40 Class XII slots. The editorial target is 9 easy, 39 medium and 12 hard questions per subject. Each paper allows 180 minutes and awards one mark per correct response, with no negative marking. The scope baseline is the official COMEDK UGET 2026 syllabus.

| Delivery check | Test 10 | Test 11 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Question paper pages | 32 | 32 |
| Worked solution pages | 35 | 34 |
| Original figures | 5 | 4 |
| Options checked in paper order | 720 | 720 |
| Sample answers independently recomputed | 24 | 26 |

All 360 new questions were matched to their syllabus anchors and checked for repeated wording against Tests 1–9 and the other new paper. There were no exact overlaps after stripping punctuation and normalising numbers. Higher-similarity pairs were reviewed for repeated reasoning. Revisions included a definite integral whose earlier version repeated the same calculation and an electrostatics question that used the same terminal calculation as an existing item. The [scope and similarity ledger](test10_11_scope_and_similarity.csv) records nearest stems, while the [angle register](../../docs/blueprints/comedk_angle_register.csv) records how the new items are solved.

The [COMEDK PYQ analysis](../../docs/blueprints/comedk_pyq_analysis.md) supplies sampled style references across the 2025 shifts. Test 11 Q043 uses field cancellation and Test 11 Q129 asks for an ordered probability under dependent draws. Test 10 Q150 uses reflection to infer an unreported integral before subtraction. Section pacing, difficulty tags and alignment to the shortest valid solution remain editorial estimates pending timed student attempts.

The review checked printed order, keys, option counts, all nine rendered figures, and parity between the canonical and delivery JSON files. The [series index](../series_index.json) lists the canonical files, HTML, PDFs and image checksums.
