# COMEDK Tests 22 and 23 review

Each mock contains 180 single-answer questions for a 180-minute clock. Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics have 60 questions each. Correct answers earn one mark. Incorrect and unattempted questions earn zero. Each section follows its 20 Class XI and 40 Class XII reservations from the [25-test plan](../../docs/blueprints/comedk_25_test_slots.csv). Syllabus checks use the [official 2026 baseline](../SOURCES.md) for provisional preparation.

All new stems were screened against Tests 1–21 and one another. The first pass found one match after punctuation and number normalisation. Review of near matches identified further repeated reasoning paths, including a thin-lens magnification calculation, two routine electrochemistry applications and an eclipsed-ethane identification prompt. The batch replaces those with distinct tasks. The final normalised stem scan found zero matches. The [scope and similarity ledger](test22_23_scope_and_similarity.csv) records each item hash, syllabus mapping, and nearest stem. The [angle register](../../docs/blueprints/comedk_angle_register.csv) records the drafted reasoning.

The [COMEDK past-paper analysis](../../docs/blueprints/comedk_pyq_analysis.md) calibrates style qualitatively using samples from the three 2025 shifts and the 2026 recall excerpts. Test 22 Q035 combines signed regions from a force trace. Test 23 Q095 tests a rotation from a Newman diagram and Q142 measures the effect of one extra feasible-region constraint. The planned difficulty split of 9 easy, 39 medium and 12 hard per subject is editorial. Student difficulty and pacing still require a timed pilot.

PDF checks matched question text, all four printed options, and the complete worked solutions against canonical JSON. The solution format includes the governing rule, working where applicable, and a separate reason for every distractor. All 12 figures were visually checked in the exported question PDFs. The [series index](../series_index.json) records the canonical and delivery checksums.
