# COMEDK Tests 12 and 13 review

Each paper has 180 single-answer questions in 180 minutes, with 60 questions each in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics. Each correct response is worth one mark, with no negative marking. The papers follow the official COMEDK UGET 2026 syllabus baseline and the planned split of 20 Class XI and 40 Class XII questions per subject.

All 360 new stems were screened against Tests 1–11 and against one another. The screen found no exact matches after punctuation and number normalisation. Review of higher-similarity pairs led to replacements in parametric differentiation, conditional probability, magnetism, and organic nomenclature. The [scope and similarity ledger](test12_13_scope_and_similarity.csv) records source-page mappings, closest bank stems and item hashes. The [angle register](../../docs/blueprints/comedk_angle_register.csv) records the reasoning used in the new questions.

The [COMEDK PYQ analysis](../../docs/blueprints/comedk_pyq_analysis.md) supplies sampled style references from the 2025 shifts. Test 12 Q003 combines a bridge balance with one short parallel-resistance calculation. Test 13 Q142 conditions on a recorded union, and Q150 is a compact definite integral by parts. The planned difficulty split of 9 easy, 39 medium and 12 hard per subject and the pacing estimates remain editorial until timed student attempts are available.

Printed answer letters match the canonical JSONs. Each solution gives the reasoning and addresses all three distractors. All eight figures were checked on their rendered question pages. The [series index](../series_index.json) records canonical, delivery and image checksums.
