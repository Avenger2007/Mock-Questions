Compare Test 2 Q082 with the afternoon shift's molecular-geometry decision, Test 3 Q133 with the afternoon shift's restricted counting, and Test 3 Q180 with the evening shift's calculus item. The recent-paper comparisons use selected questions in the archived analysis. They do not establish measured pace, official topic quotas or a comprehensive comparison against every available PYQ.

Number-normalised stems were compared across the 540 questions in COMEDK Tests 1–3. The exact check found no remaining matches. Word and character similarity surfaced candidates for a separate editorial comparison. The review also followed concepts across subjects, which caught a de Broglie mass-and-energy calculation in early Test 3 Chemistry that repeated the task in Test 2 Physics. The replacement asks what electron diffraction shows. Test 3 also replaces near-repeats in differential-equation degree, coordination number, continuity, equivalence classes and haloarene reasoning.

## Answers, diagrams and production

Every solution identifies the answer, shows the calculation or reasoning, and addresses three labelled alternatives. A separate numerical and symbolic spot check covered examples from all three subjects in both tests, including gas expansion, redox stoichiometry, optics, cell potential, signed graph area, probability, derivatives and integrals. The question and solution PDFs were checked for all 180 IDs in print order for each test. Each question paper has 720 options, each solution key matches its JSON, and each delivery JSON is byte-identical to the canonical JSON. The eight figures were inspected on their question pages. The [series index](../series_index.json) records file hashes.

## Desk pacing

Use 55 minutes for Physics, 50 for Chemistry, 66 for Mathematics and 9 for returning to flagged questions. Use about 30, 50 and 100 seconds as draft effort estimates for easy, medium and hard items. A candidate who reaches two minutes on one item should flag it and continue. The clock and budgets are the same for both papers.

The labels are the series design targets. Items that may play shorter include Test 2's interstitial-alloy explanation (Q066) and Test 3's chain-rule item (Q141). Test 3's parallel-interface ray (Q048), instantaneous rate comparison (Q064) and two-step geometry or calculus problems require additional setup. An independent full-paper timed attempt remains pending for each test. These files retain draft review status until the remaining academic and timing evidence is available.
