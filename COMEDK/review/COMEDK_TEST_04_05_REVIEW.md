# COMEDK Tests 4 and 5 review

Both are 180-minute practice drafts using the official COMEDK UGET 2026 syllabus baseline. Each paper has 60 questions per subject, one mark per correct answer and no negative marking. Each subject has 20 Class XI and 40 Class XII questions, with editorial difficulty targets of 9 easy, 39 medium and 12 hard.

| Deliverable check | Test 4 | Test 5 |
|---|---|---|
| Questions and worked explanations | 180 each | 180 each |
| Options verified in print order | 720 | 720 |
| Original figures needed to solve items | 4 | 4 |
| Question PDF / solution PDF pages | 32 / 34 | 32 / 35 |

The two papers represent 356 distinct planning anchors. The first five papers now have authored questions mapped to each of the 494 anchors in the schedule. Some anchors group multiple official syllabus phrases, so a mapping by itself does not prove that every phrase inside each group has been tested. The [item ledger](test04_05_scope_and_similarity.csv) records each question's official syllabus page, hash and closest COMEDK stem, and the [angle register](../../docs/blueprints/comedk_angle_register.csv) records the reasoning and distractor mechanisms.

The overlap check compared the 360 new items against the complete set of 900 COMEDK mock stems. Number-normalised matching found no remaining exact overlaps for the new papers. Similarity candidates were used to find recurring tasks for editorial review. Replacements include a prism question at critical incidence, distinct from Test 3's symmetric path; a neutral isotope calculation, distinct from Test 1's isobar classification; and a mobility question that now requires relating the applied voltage to field over unequal lengths. The scope pass also replaced a quadratic inequality with a linear one and kept the fluid-flow question on the interpretation of critical speed.

COMEDK style calibration uses the [recorded PYQ samples](../../docs/blueprints/comedk_pyq_analysis.md) from the 2025 morning, afternoon and evening shifts. The batch includes direct recognition, short calculations that require a model choice, and a smaller tail of multi-step questions. Relevant comparisons include Test 4 Q082 for qualitative molecular geometry and Test 5 Q157 for partial-fraction reasoning. The PYQ review is sampled.

Each answer gives the governing reasoning and a separate reason for each distractor. A separate answer check evaluated 36 numerical and symbolic examples across the six sections. All PDF question numbers, option counts and answer letters were checked against the canonical JSON. The question PDFs include the eight original figures, which were inspected after rendering. The [series index](../series_index.json) records hashes for the delivered files.

For desk pacing, allow 55 minutes for Physics, 50 for Chemistry, 66 for Mathematics and 9 for returns and checking. Editorial difficulty targets and pacing remain provisional until candidates complete independent timed attempts.
