# COMEDK Tests 14 and 15 review

Each paper has 180 single-answer questions in 180 minutes, with 60 each in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics. Each correct answer earns one mark, with no negative marking. Every subject uses 20 Class XI and 40 Class XII slots from the plan against the official 2026 COMEDK UGET syllabus.

The similarity screen compared all 360 new stems against Tests 1–13 and one another. It found no exact matches after removing punctuation and normalising numbers. Higher-similarity pairs were reviewed for shared reasoning. Replacements included repeated branch-capacitor data, a repeated inverse-trig derivative, the same change-of-scale statistics problem and a repeated transpose product. The [scope and similarity ledger](test14_15_scope_and_similarity.csv) records closest stems and syllabus-page mappings. The [angle register](../../docs/blueprints/comedk_angle_register.csv) records the reasoning and distractors for the new items.

The [COMEDK PYQ analysis](../../docs/blueprints/comedk_pyq_analysis.md) provides sampled style references across the three 2025 shifts. Test 14 Q043 uses cancellation before a short computation. Test 15 Q127 isolates a coefficient by choosing a convenient root when resolving partial fractions. Timing and the planned difficulty labels of 9 easy, 39 medium and 12 hard per subject remain editorial until timed student attempts are available.
