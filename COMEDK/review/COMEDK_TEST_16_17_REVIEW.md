# COMEDK Tests 16 and 17 review

Each paper has 180 single-answer questions in 180 minutes, with 60 each in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics. Each correct response earns one mark, with no negative marking. Each section uses 20 Class XI and 40 Class XII slots from the 25-test plan against the official COMEDK UGET 2026 syllabus.

| Delivery check | Test 16 | Test 17 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Question paper pages | 31 | 32 |
| Worked solution pages | 36 | 35 |
| Original figures | 5 | 4 |
| Options checked in paper order | 720 | 720 |
| Sample answers independently recomputed | 36 | 36 |

All 360 new stems were screened against Tests 1–15 and against one another. There were no exact matches after stripping punctuation and normalising numbers. Manual inspection of higher-similarity pairs led to replacing setups in lens magnification, geometric progressions, trigonometric identities, photoelectric data and inverse-root integration. The [scope and similarity ledger](test16_17_scope_and_similarity.csv) records syllabus-page mappings, nearest bank stems and item hashes. The [angle register](../../docs/blueprints/comedk_angle_register.csv) records the solution approach and distractors for the new items.

The [COMEDK PYQ analysis](../../docs/blueprints/comedk_pyq_analysis.md) gives sampled calibration across the three 2025 shifts. Test 16 Q051 uses a single feature of a photocurrent plot to infer the order of two frequencies. Test 17 Q161 counts two disjoint outcome classes, and Q175 separates a linear numerator into two terms with different primitives. The difficulty split of 9 easy, 39 medium and 12 hard per section and the pacing estimates remain editorial until timed attempts are available.

The printed answer keys match the canonical JSON files, and the solutions give the reasoning and address each distractor. All nine figures were checked on the rendered question pages. The [series index](../series_index.json) records the source, delivery and figure checksums.
