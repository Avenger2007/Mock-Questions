# COMEDK Tests 20 and 21 review

Each mock has 180 single-answer questions in 180 minutes, with 60 each in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics. Correct answers earn one mark. Incorrect and unattempted questions earn zero. Each subject uses 20 Class XI and 40 Class XII slots from the [25-test plan](../../docs/blueprints/comedk_25_test_slots.csv), mapped to the [official COMEDK UGET 2026 syllabus baseline](../SOURCES.md).

Screened all 360 stems against Tests 1–19 and each other. Two exact repeats found in the first draft were replaced. The final text has zero matches after punctuation and number normalisation. Closest wording and related solution paths were reviewed, including the equal-pressure drop and bubble comparison, a phase-controlled interference measurement and the allowed additions to a visual relation. The [scope and similarity ledger](test20_21_scope_and_similarity.csv) records every syllabus anchor and page, item hash and closest bank stem. The [angle register](../../docs/blueprints/comedk_angle_register.csv) records the solution and distractor reasoning.

The [COMEDK past-paper analysis](../../docs/blueprints/comedk_pyq_analysis.md) describes the qualitative calibration using sampled items across the three 2025 shifts and 2026 recalls. Test 20 Q129 links rates using two circle formulas. Test 21 Q018 reads a specified voltage across two photocurrent traces, and Q126 uses integration by parts with a short algebraic finish. The labels of 9 easy, 39 medium and 12 hard per section are editorial targets. Timing and difficulty still need student attempts.

Each PDF solution was compared with the canonical JSON. Worked solutions state the decisive rule and calculation where applicable, and explain each distractor. All 13 figures were visually checked in the rendered question PDFs. The [series index](../series_index.json) records the delivery and asset checksums.
