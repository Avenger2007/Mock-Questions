# COMEDK Tests 18 and 19 review

Each paper has 180 single-answer questions in 180 minutes, with 60 each in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics. A correct response earns one mark, with no negative marking. Each section uses 20 Class XI and 40 Class XII slots from the 25-test plan against the official COMEDK UGET 2026 syllabus baseline.

All 360 new stems were screened against Tests 1–17 and one another. There were no exact matches after stripping punctuation and normalising numbers. Higher-similarity pairs were reviewed for repeated solution paths. Edits included the capacitor-network topology, a stored-energy question, the choice of method for an inverse-trig integral and a repeated tangent-addition calculation. The [scope and similarity ledger](test18_19_scope_and_similarity.csv) records syllabus-page mappings, nearest stems and item hashes. The [angle register](../../docs/blueprints/comedk_angle_register.csv) records the reasoning used in the new items.

The [COMEDK PYQ analysis](../../docs/blueprints/comedk_pyq_analysis.md) records sampled calibration across the three 2025 shifts. Test 18 Q061 recovers an unknown original composition by comparing two pressure lowerings. Test 18 Q165 uses the counting rule with one exclusion. Test 19 Q132 combines parts with a simple rewrite of the remaining integrand. The difficulty labels of 9 easy, 39 medium and 12 hard per section and the pace remain editorial estimates pending timed student attempts.

Printed answer letters match the canonical JSON files. Each worked solution gives the reasoning and addresses all three distractors. The 15 figures were reviewed on the rendered question pages. The [series index](../series_index.json) records the canonical, delivery and figure checksums.
