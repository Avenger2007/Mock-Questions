# COMEDK Tests 8 and 9 review

Each paper contains 180 questions, with 60 each in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics. Each subject reserves 20 Class XI and 40 Class XII questions and targets 9 easy, 39 medium and 12 hard. The format remains 180 minutes, plus one mark per correct response and no negative marking. The scope baseline is the official COMEDK UGET 2026 syllabus.

The [scope and similarity ledger](test08_09_scope_and_similarity.csv) records syllabus source pages, planning anchors, item hashes and nearest matches in the COMEDK bank. The stem check screened 360 new stems against the 1,620-item COMEDK bank and found no exact matches after normalising numbers. An editorial review of overlaps replaced repeated calculations and question angles in calculus, linear systems, capacitors and chemical kinetics. The [angle register](../../docs/blueprints/comedk_angle_register.csv) records the reasoning for each new question.

The [COMEDK PYQ analysis](../../docs/blueprints/comedk_pyq_analysis.md) provides sample style references from all three 2025 shifts. Test 8 Q065 asks students to update the buffer composition before calculating the ratio, Test 8 Q175 conditions on the maximum of two ordered results, and Test 9 Q162 uses compact multistep integration. These are original practice items. Difficulty labels and the section time allocation of 55 minutes for Physics, 50 for Chemistry, 66 for Mathematics and 9 for review remain editorial estimates pending timed student attempts.

A separate calculation pass evaluated 37 sample answers across the six sections. Printed question order, option counts and answer letters matched the delivery files. All eight figures were reviewed on the rendered question pages. The [series index](../series_index.json) records canonical, delivery and image checksums.
