Answer: (A) Ascomycetes.
Why: Three clues together fix the class. The mycelium is branched and septate. Asexual spores are conidia, produced outside the cells on conidiophores. Sexual spores are formed inside sacs: these sacs are asci, the spores are ascospores, and the fruiting bodies that carry them are ascocarps. (D) Deuteromycetes are the imperfect fungi, in which only the asexual stage is known, so no sexual spores would be seen.

@@ Q04
Answer: (D) It reoxidises NADH to NAD⁺, which glycolysis needs in order to continue.
