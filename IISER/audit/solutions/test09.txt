Why not the others: (A) \(-\dfrac{3MI_0}{4R}\) (Lenz sign omitted); (B) \(\dfrac{MI_0}{4R}\) (\(I_1\) final, not \(\Delta I_1\)); (C) \(\dfrac{MI_0}{2R}\) (\(I_1(T/2)=\tfrac{I_0}{2}\)).
