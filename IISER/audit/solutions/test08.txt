Check: \[\theta=0:\ I_2=\frac{I_0}{2};\qquad\theta=90^\circ:\ I_2=0\]
Why: \(\langle\cos^2\theta\rangle=\tfrac12\ \text{for unpolarised light}\)
Why not the others: (A) \(\dfrac{I_0}{2}=I_1\) (analyser ignored); (B) \(I_0\cos^260^\circ=\dfrac{I_0}{4}\) (first \(\tfrac12\) omitted); (C) \(\dfrac{I_0}{2}\sin^260^\circ=\dfrac{3I_0}{8}\).
Key point: \(I=\dfrac{I_0}{2}\cos^2\theta\)
