(C) A conductor can be thought of as a dielectric with an infinitely large dielectric constant.
(D) Inserting a dielectric into an isolated charged capacitor increases the potential difference across it.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
A) (A) and (B) only
B) (B), (C) and (D) only
C) (A), (B) and (C) only
D) (A), (B), (C) and (D)
=
Why: (A) its molecules develop or align dipoles. (B) the induced surface charges set up a field opposing the applied one, so \(E = \dfrac{E_0}{K}\). (C) the field inside a conductor is zero, which corresponds to \(K \to \infty\).

@@ P1|Work to turn a dipole from along the field to across it|m|n|C|dipole work rotate ninety
