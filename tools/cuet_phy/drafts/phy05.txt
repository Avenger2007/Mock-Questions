@@ P1|Properties of electric field lines — which hold|m|s|C|field lines properties
Consider the following statements about electric field lines:
(A) They start on positive charges and end on negative charges.
(B) Two field lines never cross each other.
(C) Where the lines are closer together, the field is stronger.
(D) A field line is always the path followed by a free positive charge released on it.

B) (B), (C) and (D) only
C) (A), (B) and (C) only

@@ P1|Charge found from field and potential at a point|h|n|A|charge from field and potential
