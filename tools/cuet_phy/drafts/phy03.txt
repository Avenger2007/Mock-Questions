@@ P1|Potential at the centre of a square of four charges|m|n|A|potential centre square charges

A) 18 V
B) 72 V
C) 12.7 V
D) zero
=
Given: distance of each corner from the centre \(= \dfrac{\sqrt{2} \times \sqrt{2}}{2} = 1\) m

@@ P1|Capacitance with a dielectric slab filling half the gap|h|n|A|dielectric slab partial thickness

=
Given: \(C_0 = \dfrac{\varepsilon_0A}{d} = 6\) pF, \(t = \dfrac{d}{2}\), \(K = 2\)

@@ P1|Potential energy as two like charges approach|m|d|B|potential energy like charges closer

A) decreases
B) increases
C) stays the same
D) becomes zero
=

@@ P2|Series, parallel and terminal voltage — which hold|m|s|B|series same current parallel less,terminal voltage emf,ohm law diode
