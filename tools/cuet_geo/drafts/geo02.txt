(B) The Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act was passed in 1974.
(C) The Environment Protection Act was passed in 1986.
(D) Reclaimed waste-water can be used by industries for cooling and fire fighting.
A) (A), (B) and (C) only
B) (A) and (D) only
C) (B), (C) and (D) only
D) (B) and (C) only
=
Why: (B) and (C) are the two laws NCERT names, noting that they have not been implemented effectively. (D) NCERT suggests reclaimed waste-water as an attractive option for industrial cooling and fire fighting.
Why not the others: (A) is false — India has about 4 per cent of the world's water resources and about 16 per cent of its population. Options (A) and (B) include it; option (D) leaves out (D).
Key point: Recycling and reuse is one way of improving fresh water availability.
