@@ B1|Projecting population from a growth rate|h|p|A|population projection

A) 14.0 lakh
B) 16.0 lakh
C) 13.5 lakh
D) 15.0 lakh
=
Given: R grew from 9.0 to 12.0 lakh in 2001–11.
Step 1: Growth rate 2001–11 = 3 ÷ 9 × 100 = 33⅓ per cent; half of it = 16⅔ per cent.

@@ B4|Volume of rainwater harvested from a roof|m|n|B|rainwater harvesting volume calculation
A house has a flat roof of 100 square metres. The place receives 800 mm of rain in a year. If all the rain falling on the roof is harvested, how much water is collected?
A) 8,000 litres
B) 80,000 litres
C) 800 litres
D) 8,00,000 litres
=
Given: roof area = 100 m²; rainfall = 800 mm = 0.8 m.
Step 1: Volume = area × depth = 100 × 0.8 = 80 m³.
Step 2: 1 m³ = 1,000 litres, so 80 m³ = 80,000 litres.
Common mistake: forgetting to change millimetres to metres properly (8,000 or 8,00,000 litres, options A and D).
