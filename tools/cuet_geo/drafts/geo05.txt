India's minerals are not spread evenly. NCERT groups most of them into three broad belts, with some minerals found outside them.

The North-Eastern Plateau Region covers the Chotanagpur plateau of Jharkhand, the Odisha plateau, West Bengal and parts of Chhattisgarh. The South-Western Plateau Region extends over Karnataka, Goa and the contiguous uplands of Tamil Nadu and Kerala. The North-Western Region extends along the Aravalli range in Rajasthan and parts of Gujarat; its minerals are associated with the Dharwar system of rocks.

Outside these belts, the Himalayan belt has copper, lead, zinc, cobalt and tungsten in both its eastern and western parts. Assam has mineral oil deposits, and oil resources are also found off the coast near Mumbai (Mumbai High).

@@ A2|Natural growth and actual growth|m|n|B|actual growth calculation
In a year, a region recorded 30,000 births and 12,000 deaths. During the same year, 5,000 people moved in and 8,000 moved out. What was the actual growth of its population?
A) 18,000
B) 15,000
C) 21,000
D) 13,000
=
Given: births 30,000; deaths 12,000; in-migrants 5,000; out-migrants 8,000.
Step 1: Natural growth = births − deaths = 18,000.
Step 2: Actual growth = natural growth + in-migrants − out-migrants = 18,000 + 5,000 − 8,000 = 15,000.
Common mistake: stopping at natural growth (18,000, option A), or adding both migration figures (31,000) or reversing them (21,000, option C).
