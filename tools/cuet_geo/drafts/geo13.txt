The Bharmaur tribal region comprises the Bharmaur and Holi tehsils of Chamba district in Himachal Pradesh, and has been a notified tribal area since 1975. It is inhabited by the Gaddis, a tribal community who practised transhumance and speak the Gaddiyali dialect. The region has harsh climatic conditions, a low resource base and a fragile environment. Under the Fifth Five Year Plan, the tribal sub-plan was introduced in 1974, and Bharmaur became one of five Integrated Tribal Development Projects (ITDP) in Himachal Pradesh. The plan gave the highest priority to transport and communications, agriculture and allied activities, and social and community services. Female literacy rose from 1.88 per cent in 1971 to 42.83 per cent in 2001. Today only about one-tenth of households still practise transhumance, but many Gaddis migrate to Kangra and nearby areas in winter to earn a living by wage labour.
