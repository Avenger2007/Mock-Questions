(B) \(|A^{-1}|\)

Formula: corners \((0, 0)\), \((16, 0)\), \((0, 20)\) and the intersection
Step 1: \(3x + 2y = 48\), \(x + y = 20\) give \(x = 8\), \(y = 12\)
Step 2: \(Z(16, 0) = 352\), \(Z(0, 20) = 360\), \(Z(8, 12) = 176 + 216 = 392\)
Common mistake: ₹360 (A) buys only sewing machines. ₹440 (D) uses \((20, 0)\), which breaks the budget.
Key point: Write each condition as an inequality before finding corners.
