@@ C1|A² − 5A for the matrix with rows 1 2 and 3 4|m|n|A|a squared minus 5a two i
For \(A = \begin{bmatrix}1&2\\3&4\end{bmatrix}\), the matrix \(A^2 - 5A\) equals:
A) \(2I\)
B) \(-2I\)
C) \(O\)
D) \(5I\)
=
Given: \(A^2 = \begin{bmatrix}7&10\\15&22\end{bmatrix}\), \(5A = \begin{bmatrix}5&10\\15&20\end{bmatrix}\)
Formula: subtract entry by entry
Step 1: \(A^2 - 5A = \begin{bmatrix}2&0\\0&2\end{bmatrix} = 2I\)

@@ C1|Entries of one row times cofactors of another row|m|d|C|row times other row cofactors

A) \(|A|\)
B) \(1\)
C) \(0\)
D) \(-|A|\)
=
Why: This expansion is the determinant of the matrix whose second row is replaced by the first row. That matrix has two equal rows, so its determinant is 0.

Key point: Entries of one row times the cofactors of a different row always sum to 0.

B) \(k = -4\)
C) \(k = 2\)
D) \(k = 4\)
=

Common mistake: \(50\) (B) forgets the \(\dfrac{1}{2}\) in the sector area. \(\dfrac{100}{\pi}\) (C) uses a full circle of circumference 20.
Key point: Write the area in one variable using the perimeter.
