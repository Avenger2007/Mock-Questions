@@ C1|Transpose of a product|e|d|B|transpose of product
For matrices \(A\) and \(B\) for which \(AB\) is defined, \((AB)^T\) equals:
A) \(A^TB^T\)
B) \(B^TA^T\)
C) \(AB\)
D) \(BA\)
=
Why: The transpose of a product is the product of the transposes in reverse order: \((AB)^T = B^TA^T\).
