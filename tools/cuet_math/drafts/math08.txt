Food A gives 2 units of vitamin and 1 unit of mineral per kg, at ₹4 per kg. Food B gives 1 unit of vitamin and 2 units of mineral per kg, at ₹5 per kg. At least 8 units of vitamin and 10 units of mineral are needed. The least cost is:
A) ₹40
B) ₹28
C) ₹26
D) ₹32
=
Given: \(2x + y \ge 8\), \(x + 2y \ge 10\), \(x, y \ge 0\); \(Z = 4x + 5y\)
Formula: corners \((10, 0)\), \((2, 4)\), \((0, 8)\)
Step 1: \(Z = 40, 28, 40\)
Step 2: \(4x + 5y < 28\) has no point in the region, so the least cost is ₹28 at \((2, 4)\)
Common mistake: ₹40 (A) checks only the axis corners. ₹26 (C) is a value below the minimum that no feasible point reaches.
Key point: For an unbounded minimum, confirm with the half-plane test.
