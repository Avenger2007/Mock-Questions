@@ C1|Facts about the inverse of one 2 × 2 matrix|m|s|B|inverse facts statements
For \(A = \begin{bmatrix}2&-1\\1&3\end{bmatrix}\), consider the following statements:
(A) \(|A| = 7\).
(B) \(A^{-1} = \dfrac{1}{7}\begin{bmatrix}3&1\\-1&2\end{bmatrix}\).
(C) \(|A^{-1}| = \dfrac{1}{7}\).
(D) \(A\) is skew-symmetric.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
A) (A) and (B) only
B) (A), (B) and (C) only
C) (B), (C) and (D) only
D) (A), (B), (C) and (D)
=
Why: (A) \(6 - (-1)(1) = 7\). (B) \(\text{adj}\,A = \begin{bmatrix}3&1\\-1&2\end{bmatrix}\), divided by 7. (C) \(|A^{-1}| = \dfrac{1}{|A|}\).

Key point: Swap the diagonal, negate the off-diagonal, divide by the determinant.

@@ C4|Solutions, order and degree — which claims fail|m|s|D|de facts statements mixed
