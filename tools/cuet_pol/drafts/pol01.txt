# CUET Political Science Test 1 — odd key 13/12/13/12. Passage: I6 (the Emergency, 1975).

%P I6
In June 1975 the Allahabad High Court set aside Indira Gandhi's election to the Lok Sabha on an election petition filed by Raj Narain, her rival in the 1971 contest. The Supreme Court granted a partial stay: she could remain a member but could not vote in Parliament until her appeal was decided. Opposition parties, led by Jayaprakash Narayan, called for nationwide protests and asked the army, the police and government employees not to obey "illegal and immoral orders". On the night of 25 June 1975 the government advised the President to declare an emergency on the ground of "internal disturbance". The Constitution allowed this, and the declaration concentrated power in the Union government. Newspapers were censored, many opposition leaders were arrested under preventive detention laws, some organisations were banned and the right to approach the courts for the protection of fundamental rights was suspended. Parliament also passed a series of constitutional amendments. In January 1977 elections were announced, and after the Janata Party's victory a commission of inquiry was set up to examine the excesses committed during this period.
