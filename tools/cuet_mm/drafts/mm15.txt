A Hindi TV serial, Pahadon Ki Beti, is set in a village of a hill community. In its early episodes, almost every man from the community is shown drunk and lazy, and the women are shown only as superstitious. Members of the community protest on social media. They say viewers elsewhere now see them only through a few exaggerated traits, and that their children are being teased at college. A cultural organisation of the community writes to the channel.

The producers respond. They hire a researcher from the community and consult local writers. New characters are added, including a woman engineer and a schoolteacher who runs a library. Festivals and folk songs are now shown accurately, and some dialogue is spoken in the local dialect with subtitles.

Over the next season, ratings rise in the hill region. Viewers in other states write to the channel saying they have learned about the community's customs for the first time.
