Asha, a trainee reporter at a district daily, files this draft late one evening:

"The monsoon has always been a mixed blessing for the people of the valley. Farmers wait for it eagerly, and children love the first rains. This year, however, things were different. On Tuesday night, after six hours of continuous rain, the Kosra river burst its banks near Rampur village. About 400 families were moved by the district administration to relief camps in the government school. No deaths have been reported. Officials said the water level was falling by Wednesday morning."

The news editor sends it back. "The real news is buried in your fourth sentence," she says. "Open with it, answer the key questions in the first paragraph, and arrange the rest in order of importance, so that we can cut from the bottom if space runs short. Then give me a headline, and a few lines to go under Rakesh's photograph of a family wading through the water with their goats." She adds a warning: "And do not use that video of a 'collapsing bridge' going round on WhatsApp. Nobody has checked where it came from."
